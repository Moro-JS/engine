# @morojs/engine 1.1.11

A transport and hot-path release. Nothing on the wire changes; no export,
capability flag or option is added, removed or renamed.

## epoll transport: one `epoll_wait` per loop turn

Every listening and accepted socket is now a libuv poll handle on the
loop's own epoll, so libuv's single `epoll_wait` per loop turn delivers
every event; accept, receive and send remain the engine's direct calls on
plain non-blocking sockets. 1.1.10 drained a private epoll fd with a
second `epoll_wait` per turn, which showed under light or client-paced
load. Teardown is simpler as well: a socket is closed from its handle's
close callback, after libuv has released it, and the connection is freed
there. `probe()` still reports `'epoll'`.

## Engine-answered routes

Every request on a static or parameter route is parsed, validated and
routed on its own: request line and headers checked in place, framing
headers interpreted, body received, reply framed from the route's
prepared response head (rebuilt when the Date second changes) and
written. The per-connection repeat-request memo 1.1.10 introduced is
removed.

## Verified

macOS (libuv) and all three Linux transport lanes (epoll, libuv,
io_uring): 221 suite tests each, the C++ unit tests, the smoke test, no
export drift.
